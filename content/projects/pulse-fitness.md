Pulse is a fitness app designed around one idea: the workout you actually do beats the perfect workout you skip. Every screen is built to reduce friction between intention and action.

## What we did

- **Onboarding in 60 seconds** — Users reach their first workout before they're asked to commit.
- **Offline-first** — Workouts, plans, and history are available with no connection.
- **Adaptive plans** — Plans adjust to missed days instead of punishing users for them.

## Outcomes

Retention at 30 days rose to 61%, well above the 23% industry average the client benchmarked against. The app now ships on iOS and Android from a single shared codebase.
