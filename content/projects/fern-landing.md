Fern needed a website that communicated credibility in 5 seconds. We rebuilt their marketing presence around a single clear path: understand the problem, see the proof, start a conversation.

## What we did

- **Copy-first design** — Visuals serve the message rather than competing with it.
- **Performance budget** — Every page ships under 50KB of JS.
- **Accessible by default** — WCAG AA compliance verified against real assistive tech.

## Outcomes

Inbound consultation requests tripled in the quarter after launch, and the site's Core Web Vitals went all-green on every route.
