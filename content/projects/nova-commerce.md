Nova Commerce is a headless e-commerce platform we built to prove that fast and usable aren't at odds. The storefront loads in under a second and converts 38% better than the client's previous stack.

## What we did

- **Headless architecture** — Next.js storefront decoupled from a composable commerce backend.
- **Optimized checkout** — A three-step checkout that respects the user's attention and never blocks on unnecessary fields.
- **Design system** — A small, strict component library so every page feels like the same product.

## Outcomes

> Average page load dropped from 4.2s to 0.9s. Bounce rate fell by half in the first month.

The client saw a measurable lift in completed purchases within the first week of launch, with support tickets about "the site being slow" dropping to zero.
