Atlas is an internal operations dashboard for a logistics company that was drowning in spreadsheets. We rebuilt their reporting layer around the questions their teams actually ask.

## What we did

- **Information hierarchy** — Each screen leads with the one number that matters, then reveals detail on demand.
- **Real-time updates** — A streaming data layer keeps the dashboard live without page refreshes.
- **Role-based views** — Operators, managers, and execs each see a view tuned to their decisions.

## Outcomes

Time spent assembling weekly reports fell from 6 hours to 15 minutes per team. The client now ships new internal reports without touching engineering.
